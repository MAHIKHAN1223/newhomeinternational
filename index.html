Our Story

What began as a modest furnishing shop on a quiet high street has grown into something far greater. Founded on a passion for craftsmanship and a belief that every home deserves beauty, we spent years curating the finest furniture, textiles, and décor for our local community. Over time, our reputation for quality and personal service opened doors beyond borders — and New Home International was born. Today, we bring exceptional furnishing solutions to homes and spaces across the world, carrying forward the same care and attention that defined us from day one.
