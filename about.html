Our History

From New Home Furnishing to International Excellence

New Home International began its journey as New Home Furnishing — a small, dedicated shop serving the residents of Muscat with quality curtains, carpets, and upholstery. Built on a foundation of honest craftsmanship and genuine care for every customer, the business quickly earned a reputation for excellence throughout the local community.

Over the years, as demand grew and our capabilities expanded, the company evolved into New Home International — a full-spectrum luxury furnishing and interior solutions provider now serving clients across the entire Sultanate of Oman.

Today, sponsored by Mohammad Jamil SPC, we operate at an international standard, bringing the latest designs, premium materials, and cutting-edge automation technology — including our exclusive Fujikawa Japan motorized curtain rail systems — to homes, villas, offices, embassies, hotels, mosques, and commercial venues throughout Oman.

Our evolution has never compromised what made us successful from the very beginning: unwavering attention to detail, deep respect for our clients, and a genuine passion for transforming spaces into places of beauty, comfort, and enduring quality.
